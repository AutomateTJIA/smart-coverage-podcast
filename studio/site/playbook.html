Studio Playbook

How to pick and send episode topics for The Jordan Insurance Agency podcast. Read once. It takes five minutes.

Your job: find the best topics and paste the links into the studio. You can't break anything. Nothing airs until Billy reads the script and approves it. The system handles the writing, voices, scheduling and publishing.

1How to send episodes

  1. Open the studio: studio.thejordaninsuranceagency.com.
  2. Enter your agency email and the team passcode. Billy gives you the passcode. The page remembers both on your computer.
  3. Paste links, one per line, up to 14 at a time.
  4. Leave Air date blank unless Billy asked for a certain day. Blank means the next open day at 9:00 AM.
  5. Press Send to the studio. A message right under the button tells you what happened.

2What makes a good pick

  • Our own blog posts come first. Billy has already checked them, so they go straight through.
  • It answers a real question a client asks. "When do I sign up for Medicare?" beats "The history of Medicare."
  • It fits the season (see the calendar below).
  • Outside articles only from approved sources such as Medicare.gov, CMS, Social Security, HealthCare.gov, KFF, the NC Department of Insurance, Tennessee's state sites, NAIC, the Insurance Information Institute, FloodSmart, FEMA and NHTSA. The studio skips any other site.
  • Don't worry about repeats. The system refuses a link that already has an episode.

4How many to send

  • One episode airs per day, so about 5 to 7 a week keeps the calendar full.
  • Try to stay about a week ahead. Don't send a month's worth at once, because Billy has to approve each one.
  • Two on the same day only on purpose. If something important comes up, pick a date that already has an episode in Air date. The new one airs that afternoon at 3:00 PM, so the two are well apart. Otherwise it's one a day at 9:00 AM.

6When to ask Billy first

  • A site you trust that isn't on the approved list.
  • Anything about a specific plan, carrier or price. The show keeps Medicare general.
  • Breaking news or anything time-sensitive.
  • Anything you're not sure about. A quick question beats a wasted episode.

7Three don'ts

  • Don't share the team passcode outside the team.
  • Don't send pages from insurance companies or competitors.
  • Don't send articles behind a paywall or login. The studio can't read them.
