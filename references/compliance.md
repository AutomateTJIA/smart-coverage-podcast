## Classify

**EDU** (the only kind of episode we make; still needs licensed approval for Medicare/health): explains how
insurance works, rules, deadlines, comparisons of plan *types*. No plan names, carriers,
premiums, copays, or specific benefits. No steering toward enrolling.

**Out of bounds for episodes:** specific plan costs or premiums, plan benefits (dental/vision/hearing,
$0 premium, giveback), named plans or carriers, a push to enroll in a plan. If a source post
contains these, the script leaves them out. (Outside articles only. See the next paragraph for our own posts.)

**Our own blog posts are approved content (Billy, Oct 13).** Billy reviews every post before it is published, so the podcast uses the post's words, numbers and cost terms (premiums, copays, deductibles, out-of-pocket limits) as written. The editor and the final check never remove or block anything that is in the post. They only catch what the AI ADDS that is not in the post: a plan or carrier name, a dollar amount, a "$0 premium" or giveback claim, or a superlative. The strict list below still applies in full to outside articles. Trade-off Billy accepted: if a post itself names a carrier or quotes a specific plan's price, the episode will say it too, so keep posts general to keep the "no TPMO disclaimer" footing.

**Decision (Billy, Oct 11): no TPMO disclaimer in the podcast.** The show is general education. The TPMO conversation happens in appointments, not in episodes. What keeps that true: Medicare episodes stay general. No plan or carrier names, no premiums/copays/$0-premium or giveback claims, no specific plan benefits, and no push to enroll in a plan. The Episode Factory prompt and gate enforce this.

## Always

- No "free," "best," "lowest," "#1," "guaranteed" as a claim. A warning against them is fine ("a common mistake is assuming preferred means lowest cost"); the final check blocks only the claim (Oct 13)
- No misuse of the Medicare name or logo; never imply government affiliation
- No benefits that may not exist in the listener's area
- Medicare Supplement, LTC, annuity, interest-sensitive life: keep educational (NC prior-approval categories)
- State the agent is licensed; general-education disclaimer in outro and description
- AI voices (Billy's decision, Oct 13, option B): no spoken AI label. On air, the cohost
  introduces herself as "I'm Tanya, your host," and neither voice ever claims on air to be AI or
  to be a live person. In writing, every description and episode page carries
  `ai_disclosure_written` from the brand file, transcripts label her "Tanya (AI host)", the show
  description says the voices are AI-generated, and every YouTube upload ticks the "altered or
  synthetic content" box. No federal rule requires spoken labels; the FTC test is whether
  listeners are misled, and the written disclosure everywhere the show appears covers that.
- License/NPN numbers go in the description (credentials line), not on air.
- Billy's written voice-clone consent on file
