# Breakpoint Systems podcast — playbook (draft, Oct 16, 2026)

Status: the strategy is being set with Billy. Items marked **DECIDE** are open.

## Why the show exists (the money)
- Turn listeners into **blueprint ebook downloads** and **automation audit bookings** at breakpointsystems.com.
- Give buyers a way to get to know Billy and the Breakpoint method before a call.
- Show the system itself: a podcast that produces itself is a live example of what Breakpoint builds.

## Listener
Owners and founders of service businesses with 5 to 50 people who are stuck working IN the business.

## Format
- Two voices: **Billy** (host, cloned voice) and an **interviewer**. **DECIDE:** male, or a different female voice
  from the insurance show's Tanya, plus a name. Recommended role: a sharp, skeptical business owner asking the questions buyers ask.
- **Intro and outro music:** new, not the insurance show's. **DECIDE:** ElevenLabs music, royalty-free (Epidemic Sound,
  Artlist) or a custom jingle.
- Length: 5 to 7 minutes. Episode 1 can run up to 10.
- Cadence: **DECIDE** (draft: weekly, Tuesday 9:00 AM ET).
- Call to action: download the blueprint, or book an automation audit.

## Episode 1: "Start Here"
- Built from the 19-page blueprint ebook: Billy's story, the core problem (tools exist, set up badly or not at all)
  and the method at a high level.
- It does NOT read the whole ebook. It ends with "get the full blueprint at breakpointsystems.com", so the episode
  feeds the lead magnet instead of replacing it.
- Source: "Found. Called. Booked." (v11). Draft outline: `episodes/001-start-here/brief.md`.

## Topic sources
1. Monday Topic Radar email: Billy approves topics, and the system picks one each Tuesday.
2. The request form: Billy's own notes or links.
3. Later: real Breakpoint blog posts. The website blog has no real posts today; its six are archived template filler.
