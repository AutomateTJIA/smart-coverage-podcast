## Working title
"Found. Called. Booked.: How an Insurance Agency Stopped Buying Leads and Started Getting Chosen by AI"

## Outline (the interviewer asks; Billy answers)
1. **Hook (interviewer):** "What if your best customers stopped searching and started asking, and the answer named someone else?"
2. **Intro:** the show, the interviewer, Billy (owner of The Jordan Insurance Agency since 2006, founder of BreakPoint Systems).
3. **Why Billy did this:** "I didn't set out to become an AI search expert. I set out to stop paying for leads."
   Buying leads: expensive, exhausting, and five other agents call the same person before lunch.
4. **What changed:** people ask Google, ChatGPT, Copilot or their phone one question and get one answer.
   - 45% of U.S. consumers have used AI tools for local business recommendations, up from 6% a year earlier (BrightLocal, 2026).
   - About 2 billion people a month see Google's AI Overviews (Alphabet, July 2025).
   - Ten blue links became a short list of two or three names. "Ranking used to mean page one. Now it means being in the answer."
5. **SEO vs AEO in plain English:** SEO ranks a page; AEO gets your business named in the answer. Not a trick, not
   "just add an FAQ page", and not only for big brands.
6. **How AI decides who to recommend (the five patterns):** clear answers to real questions, depth on your subject,
   proof from other places, consistency everywhere, freshness and easy access. Plus: your website has to win the call.
7. **What they did at the agency (short version):**
   - fixed the website first
   - swapped the contact form for a calendar
   - researched what customers ask
   - published on a plan
   - measured and filled the gaps ("feeding the monster")
   - earned trust beyond the site
   - an AI receptionist for the calls
   - no ads and no bought leads
8. **The results (platform reports, with the caveat said out loud):**
   - Bing AI citations up 575% from the first to the last 30 days
   - 10,071 total Bing AI citations, July 8 to Oct 5, 2026
   - Google clicks up 10.8 times, June to September 2026
   - $0 on paid ads
   - 5 to 7 new-lead conversations a day
   - **Required line:** these are The Jordan Insurance Agency's own results, in its market. They're not typical, and every business is different.
9. **The part nobody tells you:** done by hand, it's close to a 40-hour-a-week job. Software alone won't do it; it has
   to be set up around your business. "Trial and error got us here. Systems are how we stayed here."
10. **Why inbound beats bought:** a bought lead is rented attention; a recommendation is something you own. "Ads are rent. Visibility is ownership."
11. **Recap (one line):** three things to remember.
12. **CTA:** get the full guide, with the 10-question scorecard and the seven questions to ask before you hire anyone,
    at breakpointsystems.com, or book a free 30-minute Discovery Call.
13. **Outro and close.**
